Grad-CAM Explanation

The heatmap below highlights the regions of the tissue image that most influenced the model's prediction. The colours in the heatmap represent the following:

  • Red / Orange: regions that strongly influenced the prediction (high importance)
  • Yellow / Green: regions with moderate influence on the prediction
  • Blue / Dark Blue: regions with little or no influence on the prediction

Note: If the heatmap appears mostly dark blue, this indicates the model made its prediction with very high confidence and the gradient signal is near zero, which is a known characteristic of gradient-based explanation methods at high confidence levels.

About This System

Deployed Model

DenseNet121: selected from a comparative analysis of VGG16, ResNet50, DenseNet121 and InceptionV3 based on highest accuracy and ROC-AUC across all magnifications.

Dataset

BreaKHis: Breast Cancer Histopathological Image Classification dataset. All four magnification levels supported: 40X, 100X, 200X, 400X.

Explainable AI

Grad-CAM (Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping) generates visual heatmaps to explain which tissue regions influenced the model's prediction.

Classification

Binary classification (Benign or Malignant) with a confidence score for each prediction.
